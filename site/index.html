Plan your day, week & month.

OpenTodo is a free planner that keeps your daily tasks, weekly goals and monthly plans in one calm place. Your data stays on your device — and you can plug in your own AI.

Features

Looks simple. Does a lot.

Move between your day, week and month; keep routines going, focus, and watch your progress.

Day · Week · Month

Today's list, a weekly pool and monthly goals. Move a task to a day, week or month in one click — "Balance my week" spreads work over your freest days.

Routines & streaks

Daily, weekly, monthly or chosen-weekday tasks. A 30-day calendar and streaks show how your habits are going.

Focus timer

A Pomodoro timer tied to a task — it logs the time and keeps the subtasks next to you.

Type to add

Time, length, tag and repeat in one line:

14:00 call #work ~30m@tomorrow%daily!urgent

Private by default

No account, no server. Everything is stored on your device. Export a JSON backup or pick a file for automatic backups.

Analytics

Completion rates, a 28-day heatmap, tag and priority breakdowns.

A real desktop app

Runs quietly in the tray, sends reminders for timed tasks and can start with Windows.

Two languages

English and Turkish, one click apart. Dates and day names follow along.
